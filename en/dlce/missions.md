# Missions
<!-- last-modified --> 

> Gameplay

## Mission rules
The game offers 5 missions, of which the first 2 are fixed and the remaining 3 refresh every 24 hours. Each completed mission rewards you with stars or energy cubes.

## Mission list

> [!TIP]
> The table lists all missions for reference

| Mission objectives                               | Rewards             |
|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Log in to the game（Fixed the First）**         | **500 Stars✨**     |
| **Complete any level 1 times（Fixed the Second）**| **100 Stars✨**     |
| **Complete any level 3 times**                   | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **Consume 200 stars**                            | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **Collect 20 diamonds in any level**             | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **Collect 6 crowns in any level**                | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **Die 10 times in any times**                    | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **revive 5 times in any level**                  | **200 Stars✨**     |
| **Consume 15 cubes**                             | **20 Energy Cubes** |

## Additions
- “Missions”is at the top of the main menu, and you can also access it from the [Stars](/dlce/coins.md#star) interface and [Energy Cubes](/dlce/coins.md#cube) interface.
- Complete missions are greyed out and muted.
